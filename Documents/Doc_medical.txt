DOCUMENT 1
Title: Hypertension Management Protocol
Department: Cardiology
Effective Date: January 2026
Overview: This protocol outlines the primary treatment path for Stage 1 hypertension (130-139/80-89 mmHg).
Lifestyle Interventions: All patients must first attempt a 3-month period of lifestyle modification, including the DASH diet and 150 minutes of weekly aerobic exercise.

Policy Details:
If blood pressure remains elevated after 3 months, initiate low-dose ACE inhibitors or Calcium Channel Blockers. 
Patients should perform home blood pressure monitoring twice daily (morning and evening) and log results.

DOCUMENT 2
Title: Pediatric Fever and Ibuprofen Safety
Department: Pediatrics
Revision: 4.0
Dosage Standards: Ibuprofen may be administered to infants older than 6 months for fever reduction.

Safety Restrictions:
The standard dose is 10mg/kg every 6 to 8 hours, not to exceed 4 doses in 24 hours. 
Do not administer ibuprofen to infants under 6 months old or those with suspected dehydration due to risk of kidney injury.

DOCUMENT 3
Title: Annual Physical and Preventative Screening
Department: General Medicine
Policy Year: 2026
General Screening: Healthy adults aged 18–45 should undergo a comprehensive physical exam once every two years.

Screening Exceptions:
Routine fasting lipid panels and glucose checks are mandatory during these biennial visits. 
Patients with pre-existing chronic conditions (e.g., Diabetes, Asthma) are exempt from this timeline and must be seen at least every 6 months.

DOCUMENT 4
Title: Emergency Triage: Acute Respiratory Distress
Department: Emergency Medicine
Status: High Priority
Immediate Action: If a patient presents with a pulse oximetry reading below 92%, immediate supplemental oxygen must be administered regardless of prior history.

Emergency Override:
Standard "wait and see" approaches for mild asthma are suspended if the patient exhibits "silent chest" or accessory muscle use. 
Vital signs must be recorded every 5 minutes until the patient is stabilized or transferred to the ICU.

Title: Employee Professional Development Fund
Department: Talent & Culture
Effective: Fiscal Year 2026
Annual Stipend: Each full-time employee is eligible for a $2,500 annual stipend for certifications, conferences, or tuition reimbursement.

Expense Rules:

DOCUMENT 8
Title: Password and Authentication Standard
Department: Information Security
Version: 4.2
Password Complexity: Passwords must be at least 16 characters long and include a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.

Auth Protocols:
Mandatory password changes are required every 90 days for all administrative accounts. 
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is strictly required for all remote logins and access to sensitive financial databases.

DOCUMENT 9
Title: Regional Shipping and Logistics
Department: Supply Chain
Revision Date: February 2026
Standard Delivery: Domestic orders are shipped via ground transport with an expected delivery window of 5–7 business days.

Shipping Constraints:
Any shipment containing lithium-ion batteries must be labeled "Class 9 Miscellaneous" and is ineligible for air transport.

DOCUMENT 10
Title: Diabetic Retinal Screening
Department: Ophthalmology
Effective Date: January 2026
Standard: All patients diagnosed with Type 2 Diabetes must receive a dilated eye exam annually to screen for retinopathy.

Urgency Details:
If a patient reports sudden "floaters" or flashes of light, they must be seen by a specialist within 24 hours.

DOCUMENT 11
Title: Controlled Substance Prescription Policy
Department: Pharmacy Compliance
Refill Rules: Schedule II controlled substances cannot be refilled; a new prescription is required for every dispensing event.

Safety Protocols:
Patients must present a valid government-issued photo ID to collect any narcotic medication.

DOCUMENT 12
Title: Telehealth Eligibility
Department: Patient Services
Clinical Criteria: Initial diagnostic consultations for neurological symptoms must be conducted in person; follow-ups may occur via video.

Patient Requirements:
Patients are responsible for ensuring a private, quiet environment for the duration of the 20-minute session.

DOCUMENT 13
Title: Post-Operative Wound Care
Department: Surgical Nursing
Maintenance: Surgical sites must remain dry for the first 48 hours; do not submerge in water (baths or pools).

Infection Signs:
Notify the surgical team immediately if the site temperature exceeds 101°F or if yellow discharge is present.

Title: Hospital Visitor Safety
Department: Facilities
Visiting Hours: Standard visiting hours are 08:00 to 20:00 daily.

Capacity Restrictions:
No more than two visitors are permitted in a double-occupancy room at any single time.

DOCUMENT 15
Title: Anticoagulation Therapy (Warfarin)
Department: Hematology
Dietary Management: Patients must maintain a consistent intake of Vitamin K (leafy greens) to avoid fluctuations in INR levels.

Emergency Indicators:
Uncontrolled nosebleeds lasting longer than 15 minutes require immediate ER evaluation.

Department: Maternity

DOCUMENT 17

Department: Radiology
Contraindications: Patients with non-MRI-compatible pacemakers or metallic fragments in the eye are strictly prohibited from the magnet room.

Department: Immunology
